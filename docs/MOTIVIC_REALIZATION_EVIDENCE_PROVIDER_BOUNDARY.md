# Motivic Realization Evidence Provider Boundary

**Status:** `FUTURE_RESEARCH_ONLY`  
**Authority:** `NONE`  
**Scientific execution:** `NONE`  
**Claim promotion:** `NONE`

## 1. Purpose

Preserve a future Formal Discovery seam for producing, normalizing, or custody-binding mathematical evidence that ONTO or another observer may later consume when studying motivic realizations, cohomological filtrations, monodromy, transport, and categorical reformulations.

Formal Discovery may eventually provide bounded evidence packages. It does not thereby own the scientific interpretation of recurrence, and it does not gain authority to claim the Hodge Conjecture or any other major conjecture.

## 2. Ownership Split

```text
FORMAL_DISCOVERY:
  construct / qualify / custody-bind bounded formal evidence

ONTO:
  measure recurrence / invariance across observers

PROOF OR MATHEMATICS PROVIDER:
  supply theorem-level authority where required
```

Permanent firewall:

```text
PROVIDER_EVIDENCE_ACCEPTED
!=
ONTO_HYPOTHESIS_SUPPORTED
```

## 3. Candidate Evidence Families

Future provider contracts may include bounded records for:

- realization functors and comparison maps;

- algebraic-support / coniveau data;
- local systems and monodromy representations;

No family is activated by this document.

## 5. Object Versus Morphism Boundary

A realization contract must state whether it concerns:

- an object;
- a morphism;
- a cohomology class;
- a filtration;
- a support condition;
- a comparison isomorphism;
- a functorial compatibility statement.

Permanent firewalls:

```text
OBJECT_REALIZATION
!=
MORPHISM_LIFT

CONSERVATIVE_FUNCTOR
!=
FULL_FUNCTOR

ESSENTIAL_IMAGE_OBJECT
!=
SPECIFIC_CLASS_IN_IMAGE
```

Provider outputs must preserve this scope explicitly.

## 6. Coefficient and Base-Field Custody

Every cohomological or motivic record must bind its coefficient domain and relevant base field or geometric setting.

Do not silently move between:

```text
Z
Q
R
C
```

or between characteristic-zero and positive-characteristic settings.

Coefficient/base-field data are part of the evidence identity.

## 7. Filtration Evidence

Future filtration packages should distinguish at minimum:


A provider may compute or bind a filtration profile, but ONTO decides separately whether that profile recurs beyond null expectation.

## 14. Nonclaims

This document does not establish that:

- Formal Discovery can construct algebraic cycles from Hodge classes;
- persistence determines coniveau;
- monodromy invariance implies algebraicity;
- a Hodge realization is full or conservative on any target category;
- motivic evidence proves the Hodge Conjecture;
- noncommutative reformulation proves the classical conjecture;
- any backend or theorem provider is qualified;
- any new experiment is authorized.

It preserves a future evidence-provider boundary so that later ONTO studies can consume exact, scoped, provenance-bound mathematical records without conflating provider authority with empirical recurrence.
