- conjugacy-class tuples;
- Nielsen classes;
- braid/Hurwitz actions;
- covers and Hurwitz spaces;
- local monodromy;
- arithmetic Galois actions;
- numerical Belyi reconstruction;
- coordinate/representation selection;
- exact algebraic recognition;
- descent and exact certification.

The mathematical target is inverse Galois theory, not the differential-Galois target used by the Hamiltonian variational program.

## Relation to the Hamiltonian variational-Galois program

No mathematical transfer from `M23` to CAND-1 is claimed.

The relevant methodological question is narrower:

> When an exact provider becomes computationally intractable on a higher-degree algebraic NVE, should the next action be larger compute budget, another provider, or a mathematically equivalent representation better adapted to the solver?

This intake recommends that future timeout successors evaluate exact representation/coordinate alternatives before brute-force budget escalation.

Candidate search space:

- singularity-adapted Möbius coordinates;
- factorized rational coefficient forms;
- symmetry-adapted variables;
- algebraic pullbacks;
- parity-adapted decompositions where exact equivalence is established;
- alternate exact second-order normal forms;
- independently qualified exact providers.

Any transformation used in the differential-Galois program must preserve the relevant identity-component predicate under an explicitly qualified equivalence theorem or exact derivation.
