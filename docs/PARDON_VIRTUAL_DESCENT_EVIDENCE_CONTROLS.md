### PARDON-CONTACT-HOMOLOGY-2019

- John Pardon, *Contact homology and virtual fundamental cycles*.
- Journal of the American Mathematical Society 32, 2019, 825-919.
- DOI: `10.1090/jams/924`.
- Evidence class: `PRIMARY_THEOREM_SOURCE`.
- Qualified relevance: construction of contact homology using coherent virtual fundamental-cycle machinery for the paper's moduli spaces.
- Required firewall: arbitrary higher-codimension singularities are not thereby contact-type or Reeb-asymptotic.

### GPS-MICROLOCAL-MORSE-2024

- Sheel Ganatra, John Pardon, Vivek Shende, *Microlocal Morse theory of wrapped Fukaya categories*.
- Annals of Mathematics 199(3), 2024, 943-1042.
- DOI: `10.4007/annals.2024.199.3.1`.
- arXiv: `1809.08807`.
- Evidence class: `PRIMARY_THEOREM_SOURCE`.
- Qualified relevance: under the stated real-analytic/subanalytic hypotheses, equivalence between the stopped partially wrapped Fukaya category of `T* M` and compact objects in an unbounded derived category of sheaves on `M` with microsupport constraint.
- Required firewall: this theorem does not supply a functor from analog QPU control perturbations to Detector Error Models.
